Wedding Q&A
Frequently Asked Questions
1. Can I bring a plus one?
Yes, you’re welcome to bring a plus one! Just make sure to include their name when you RSVP.
2. Are kids allowed at the wedding?
Yes! Feel free to bring your whole crew! Please help maintain a quiet atmosphere during church rites.
3. What time should I arrive?
We recommend arriving 45 minutes before the ceremony starts.
4. Can I take photos or post on social media?
Feel free to capture photos or videos throughout the ceremony! Please tag us with #agtgth so we can cherish these special moments together.
5. Will there be parking at the venue?
Limited on-site parking around the cathedral (approx. 20-30 cars). Public transit via BTS Saphan Taksin (Exit 3) or express boat is recommended.
